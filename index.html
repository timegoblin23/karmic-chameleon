October 2026 · Meta, Google and store

The ads got better. The spend got smaller.

October delivered £9,929 on £1,490 of ad spend. Revenue is 51% below June because investment is 59% below June — not because Meta stopped working. Measured against last October, the store is up 42% and profit is up 62%.

01

The short answer

  • Meta is the strongest it has been. On £770 it returned £5,017 at 6.52 ROAS — 4.9× the return on almost the same spend last October, and better than any month this year.
  • The gap to June is investment and mix, not performance. Blended spend fell from £3,608 to £1,490. Google accounts for £5,101 of the revenue gap, Meta £3,560 and email £684.
  • October is not deteriorating. Revenue per day is £397, ahead of September's £374. The month is tracking to roughly £12,300.
02

Revenue has tracked spend all year

June was the peak of investment as well as revenue: £20,279 on £3,608 of spend. As budget came out through the summer, revenue followed — but return rose. Blended ROAS went from 6.05 in June to 7.39 in September and 7.03 in October.

04

Why June was the peak

  • It was the last full-funnel month. Meta £1,966 plus Google £1,642 — £3,608 invested, 2.4× October's £1,490.
  • Google was doing as much work as Meta. It returned £8,586 at 5.23 ROAS in June; it now runs at 45% of that spend and returned £3,485.
  • Email added £1,069. It added £385 in October — a 64% fall and the biggest proportional decline in the account.
  • The hero product was in stock and the creative was varied. Multiple campaigns and batches were live. Today a single catalogue ad at £30/day carries the whole account.
  • The year-on-year comparison flatters June. It is measured against a much smaller base. October 2025 is the fairer like-for-like, and this October is 42% ahead of it.

06

The decision

Stopping now would remove the channel producing half of store revenue at the best return we have recorded. The better move is to give it something to scale.

  1. 1
    Approve the £20/day test
    Three new angles alongside the catalogue ad, run for two weeks against it. This is the smallest version of the refresh — and the one already waiting on approval.
  2. 2
    Put Google back to work
    Restore £1,200–1,600 a month at a 4× ROAS floor. It returned 4.71 on £739 in October and 5.23 in June; the demand is there, the budget is not.
  3. 3
    Rebuild the email cadence
    Get back to June's rhythm. Even half of June's £1,069 is about £500 a month at no media cost — the highest-margin revenue available.
